Privacy Policy

App: NotRealFactory Tycoon · Developer: WaskeSoo Games
Last updated: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WaskeSoo Games ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game NotRealFactory Tycoon (the "Game"). By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

Information we and our partners collect

The Game itself does not require you to create an account and does not ask you for your name, email, or contact details. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

To show advertisements, third-party advertising partners integrated in the Game may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

  • your device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID);
  • device information (model, operating system version, language, country, network type);
  • IP address and coarse (country-level) location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, rewards granted).

How the information is used

  • to display advertisements, including rewarded ads you choose to watch;
  • to measure ad performance and prevent fraud;
  • where you consent, to show personalized (interest-based) advertising.

Advertising partners

We use the following advertising and mediation services. Their own privacy policies describe how they process data:

  • Unity Ads / Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies) — privacy policy
  • Google AdMob / Google User Messaging Platform (Google) — privacy policy and list of ad partners

Your consent choices (EEA / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the Game shows a consent request before serving personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time from the Game's settings ("Privacy options"). If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g. under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or object to its processing. Because we do not hold account data ourselves, most such requests concerning ad data should be directed to the advertising partners listed above. You may also reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Data collected by advertising partners is retained according to their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
