Privacy Policy

App: NotRealFactory Tycoon · Developer: WaskeSoo Games
Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WaskeSoo Games ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game NotRealFactory Tycoon (the "Game"). By playing the Game you agree to this policy.

Information we and our partners collect

The Game itself does not require you to create an account and does not ask you for your name, email, or contact details. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

Google Play Games (optional sign-in)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the following features use Google Play Games Services:

  • Cloud save — a copy of your game progress is stored in the hidden app folder of your own Google Drive, so you can restore it on another device. We cannot access your Drive.
  • Global leaderboards — your reputation score and the company name you choose in the Game are sent to Google Play Games and shown to other players, together with your Play Games player name and avatar if your Play Games profile is visible. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as the company name.
  • Google provides us with a game-specific player ID to link your progress and scores to your Play Games profile.

Signing in is optional; the Game is fully playable without it. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

To show advertisements, third-party advertising partners integrated in the Game may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

  • your device advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / GAID);
  • device information (model, operating system version, language, country, network type);
  • IP address and coarse (country-level) location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, rewards granted).

How the information is used

  • to display advertisements, including rewarded ads you choose to watch;
  • to measure ad performance and prevent fraud;
  • where you consent, to show personalized (interest-based) advertising.

Advertising partners

We use the following advertising and mediation services. Their own privacy policies describe how they process data:

  • Unity Ads / Unity LevelPlay, including ironSource Ads (Unity Technologies) — privacy policy
  • Google AdMob / Google User Messaging Platform (Google) — privacy policy and list of ad partners

Your consent choices (EEA / UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the Game shows a consent request before serving personalized ads. You can change or withdraw your choices at any time from the Game's settings ("Privacy options"). If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g. under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or object to its processing. Because we do not hold account data ourselves, most such requests concerning ad data should be directed to the advertising partners listed above. You may also reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Cloud saves and leaderboard scores remain in your Google account until you delete them (see below). Data collected by advertising partners is retained according to their own policies.

How to delete your data

NotRealFactory Tycoon (by WaskeSoo Games) does not create its own user accounts, so there is no separate account to close. You can delete the data associated with the Game at any time:

  1. Game progress on your device — uninstall the Game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → NotRealFactory Tycoon → Storage → Clear data.
  2. Cloud save — open Google Drive on the web → Settings (gear icon) → Manage apps → NotRealFactory Tycoon → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  3. Leaderboard scores and Play Games data — in your Google Play Games settings choose “Delete Play Games account & data” and delete the data for NotRealFactory Tycoon only, or your whole Play Games profile.
  4. Advertising data — reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads), and withdraw consent in the Game's settings (“Privacy options”, EEA/UK/CH).
  5. By email — write to waskesoogames@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion – NotRealFactory Tycoon” and we will guide you through the steps above or forward your request to our partners. We reply within 30 days.

Deleting data with the steps above removes it permanently; nothing is kept by us. Advertising partners may keep limited data where required by law (e.g. fraud prevention or accounting), according to their own policies.
